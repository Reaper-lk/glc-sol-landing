About the initiative

One community.
Two ecosystems.

The Solana token is designed to introduce Goldcoin to a broader on-chain audience while supporting the continued development of the native Goldcoin ecosystem.

It does not replace the native Proof-of-Work GLC blockchain. It is a separate Solana-based community token intended to create new visibility, liquidity opportunities, and sustainable project funding.
